## Project Overview

SkillTrail is a digital product concept designed to help university students track their career development in one place.

The project explores how students currently manage their projects, certificates, skills, and career goals, identifies the challenges they face, and defines an opportunity for improvement through UX research.

## Research Objectives

- Understand how students currently track their projects, certificates, and skills.
- Identify challenges in monitoring career progress.
- Explore what students need to feel more confident about their career readiness.
- Identify opportunities to improve the student career-tracking experience.

## Research Process

The project followed these steps:

1. **Research Planning:** Defined research goals and questions.
2. **Secondary Research:** Reviewed published student surveys and hiring trends.
3. **Competitive Analysis:** Evaluated LinkedIn, Coursera, Handshake, and Notion/spreadsheets.
4. **Proto-Personas & JTBD:** Developed two fictional user profiles based on research assumptions.
5. **Affinity Mapping:** Organized observations into four key themes.
6. **Problem Definition:** Formulated a user-centered problem statement and How Might We questions.

## Key Research Findings

Four main themes emerged from the research:

- **Scattered Proof:** Students keep certificates and projects across multiple platforms.
- **Unclear Progress:** Students struggle to understand how far they have progressed toward their career goals.
- **Uncertainty About Next Steps:** Students may not know which skills to develop next.
- **Difficulty Sharing Achievements:** Students find it challenging to present their strongest work to recruiters and mentors.

### Key Insight

Students don't lack effort. They lack a clear picture of their own progress.

## Proto-Personas

Two fictional personas were created to represent potential users:

- **Nour Hassan:** A third-year Computer Science student who wants to build a strong portfolio before applying for internships.
- **Omar Khaled:** A final-year Business student who wants to present clear evidence of his skills when applying for graduate jobs.

**Note:** These are proto-personas based on research assumptions, not validated user profiles.

## Problem Statement

University students collect projects, certificates, and skills in many different places. Because they cannot see their progress in one view, they feel unsure about their career readiness and struggle to decide what to do next.

### How Might We...

- Help students see all their progress in one place?
- Turn career goals into small, clear next steps?
- Make it easier to share their best work?

## Assumptions to Validate

- Students keep evidence of their work across multiple platforms.
- Seeing their progress in one place could increase motivation.
- Students want to share their progress with mentors and recruiters.
